# Daily Lesson Creator

Create a 30-minute lesson in the target language focused on one of: grammar,
speaking, listening comprehension, vocabulary, pronunciation, writing.

## Steps

1. **Fix the focus.** The learner names a focus or a topic. Map it to one of
   the six focus areas. One lesson, one focus.
2. **Set the objective.** One sentence: what the learner can do after the
   lesson that they couldn't before.
3. **Build the 30-minute arc.** Use the 10/10/5/5 split as a guideline rather
   than a rigid requirement:
   - **Warm-up** (~10 min) — a quick activation: recall the session's words,
     a one-line question, a sound or pattern to notice.
   - **Core** (~10 min) — explain the focus point with at least three
     practical, real-life examples in the target language, graded to the
     level and kept *comprehensible*.
   - **Practice** (~5 min) — exercises that climb: recognition first, then
     production. In the target language where the focus allows.
   - **Quiz** (~5 min) — a short 3–5 question quiz; check answers immediately.

   Adjust the balance to fit the requested focus while keeping the total
   lesson around 30 minutes. A speaking lesson can stretch practice time; a
   pronunciation lesson can shrink theory.

4. **Close** with one takeaway sentence and one thing to review tomorrow.

## Completion

The lesson is complete when it has all of: one objective, an explanation,
practical examples, exercises, and a quiz with answers — whatever the time
balance. The quiz is the retrieval check; a lesson without one is incomplete.
